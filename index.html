About Us

StudySphere is an independent educational platform created to help MPSC Combined Group B & C aspirants practice objective questions topic-wise.

Our focus is on structured practice, topic-wise quizzes, practice tests, quick revision, detailed explanations, and bilingual learning (Marathi & English).

Disclaimer: StudySphere is an independent initiative and is not affiliated with the official Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC).

Privacy Policy

At StudySphere, your privacy is respected. Since this is a static educational website:

  • No mandatory account creation or login is required.
  • Local browser storage (localStorage) is used solely to remember your UI preferences (Dark mode, Font size) and quiz progress locally on your device.
  • Question reports are stored locally in your browser.
  • We do not collect, process, or sell personal data.
  • External links (like Telegram) have their own privacy policies.
  • Donations are processed directly through your UPI application, and we do not collect payment data.
